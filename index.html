Simplilearn · AI for live operations · Live in production

Control Centre — AI-monitored live classes

An LLM-assisted live-class intelligence platform that analyses Zoom chat and session events in real time, detects learner-impacting issues, calculates session health and risk, and tells operators which class needs them next.

Ops staffing & cost −40% 12+ concurrent sessions per operator LLM + rule engine hybrid
  • Defined the hybrid AI framework: LLM classification, keyword detection, deterministic rules, confidence handling, and human-in-the-loop resolution workflows.
  • Enabled a single operator to supervise 12+ concurrent sessions, concentrating attention on the ~10% of classes flagged at risk.
  • The result: a 40% reduction in live-class operations staffing and cost.
